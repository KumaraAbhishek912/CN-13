WIRESHARK LAB EXPERIMENT

AIM
To study Wireshark for packet capture, filtering, protocol analysis, and network troubleshooting.

PROCEDURE
1. Open Wireshark and select the active Wi-Fi or Ethernet interface.
2. Start packet capture and generate ordinary network traffic.
3. Stop and save the capture as wireshark_capture.pcapng.
4. Apply filters such as tcp, udp, dns, and tls.
5. Inspect packet details including source, destination, protocol, and length.
6. Open Statistics and view Protocol Hierarchy, Endpoints, and Conversations.
7. Save screenshots of your actual results.

OUTPUT
Add actual screenshots of packet capture, display filtering, Protocol Hierarchy, and Endpoints.

RESULT
Complete after performing the experiment and checking your results.

NOTE
Capture only traffic you are authorized to inspect. Check captures for sensitive information before uploading to a public repository.
